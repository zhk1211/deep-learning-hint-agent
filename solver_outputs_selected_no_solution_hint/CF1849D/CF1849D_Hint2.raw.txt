To paint a zero on one side, we need at least 1 charge available at that boundary. Since we can direct the charge, we can paint a zero on a side if S - (length - 1) >= 1? But we also need to consider that we might want to paint zeros on both sides. The total charge needed for external zeros is the number of zeros we paint. So if we want to paint L zeros on left and R zeros on right (each 0 or 1), we need S - (length - 1) >= L + R. But also, we need to be able to allocate the charge to the correct boundaries. Since we can always choose the starting paid element to leave charge on the desired boundaries, as long as the total remaining charge is at least the number of external zeros we want to paint, and we only paint at most one per side, it should be possible?
