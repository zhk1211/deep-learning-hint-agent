            // - Moves 1..m-1 are leaf moves. They are made by alternating players starting with Iris.

            // The player who moves first after root is the opponent of the one who made move m.

            // If Dora makes root move (m even), then Iris starts the after-root leaf moves.

            // If R=0: score = (number of 1s among fixed leaves after all moves) = b + (1s added before root) + (1s added after root).
